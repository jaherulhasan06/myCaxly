Privacy Policy for Caxly

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Caxly ("the app", "we", "our") is an offline-first expense, notes and reminders app for Android, developed by Jaherul Hasan. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, which permissions it uses, and what happens when you choose to export, share, or open an external payment app.

In short: Caxly does not collect, transmit, or share your data. Everything you enter stays on your device, encrypted, unless you personally choose to export or share it.

1. Data we collect

Caxly collects none of your personal or financial data on any server. We do not operate a backend, there is no account or login, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

The information you enter is stored only in an encrypted local database on your device (SQLCipher, AES-256). This includes:

  • Expense categories ("cards"), purchase item names, prices, dates, monthly archives and expense cycles.
  • Notes (titles and content).
  • Reminders: title, optional amount, due date and time, optional notes, priority, category, recurrence, completion status and notification settings.
  • App settings, such as theme, App Lock timeout and screenshot-protection choice.
  • A small local preference record for payment apps you have opened from Caxly: the app's Android package name, a usage count and a last-used time, used only to order the payment-app list by recent use.

None of this leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it (see Section 4).

2. Permissions the app uses, and why

Permission / capabilityPurpose
Biometric / device credentialOptional App Lock. Authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system. Caxly receives only a pass/fail result and never sees or stores your fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password.
NotificationsTo show reminders you create and optional purchase reminders. A reminder notification can show the reminder's title and, if you entered one, its amount. Notifications are scheduled and delivered locally by Android; there is no remote notification server.
Exact alarms, full-screen alerts, vibrationSo time-sensitive reminders can fire at the time you set and, for high-priority reminders, appear as an alarm-style alert. If Android does not grant exact alarms, Caxly falls back to standard local scheduling.
Receive boot completedTo restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts or the app is updated.
Query specific installed appsOnly to detect which UPI/payment apps (for example Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, FamPay) are installed so Caxly can let you choose and open one. Caxly does not request a list of all installed apps, does not read data from these apps, and cannot see your payment activity inside them.

Caxly does not declare an internet permission and does not connect to any server of its own. Opening a website or email link from Settings hands off to your browser or email app.

3. Payments

Caxly does not process payments and is not a payment app. It only opens a UPI app you already have installed. Caxly does not build a payment request containing your expense amount or a merchant's details, and never stores, sees, or transmits your payment credentials, UPI PIN, bank details or card numbers. Once a payment app is open, it controls the payment and is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Exporting, sharing and importing your own data

You can move data out of Caxly at any time:

  • JSON backup: a full backup that can include your categories, purchases, archives, cycles, notes, reminders and applicable settings, for restoring on the same or a new device.
  • CSV export: a spreadsheet-friendly export of purchases.
  • PDF settlement and text summaries: shareable settlement reports.

These files are generated on your device and leave it only if you choose to share them (for example via WhatsApp, email or Google Drive) through Android's share sheet. The destination you choose is responsible for how it handles that information. We, the developer, never receive a copy.

Restoring a JSON backup uses Android's file picker: Caxly reads only the file you select. Importing is a confirmed restore and can replace the corresponding current data in the app, so export a backup first if you want to keep what you have now.

Generated backup and CSV files are kept in app-private storage and are automatically deleted after a day. A file you save or share outside Caxly is no longer under Caxly's control.

5. Data storage and security

  • Your database is encrypted at rest with SQLCipher (AES-256). The encryption key is generated on your device and stored using Android Keystore-backed secure storage.
  • Caxly opts out of Android's automatic cloud and device backup, so your data is not silently copied off your device by the operating system. Use the in-app backup export to move data between devices.
  • Optional screenshot and screen-recording protection can be switched on or off in Settings. When on, it also hides the app's preview in the Recent Apps screen.
  • No system is perfectly secure. A device that is rooted or physically compromised may be able to bypass protections that apps rely on.

6. Data retention and deletion

Caxly keeps your data on your device until you delete it. You can delete records inside the app, clear Caxly's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, which permanently removes the local data. Because we hold no server-side copy, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

7. Third parties

Caxly does not share data with any third party, advertiser, or analytics provider, and does not sell personal information, because it does not collect any to share. Apps and services you choose to open or share to (payment apps, messaging apps, email, cloud storage, websites) operate under their own policies.

8. Children's privacy

Caxly is a general personal-finance utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and per Section 1, we do not collect data from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
